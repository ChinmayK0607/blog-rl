# 1.7B constrained diagnostic note

Matched constrained 24-game run: broadcasts 960/960 strict-valid and grounded; actions 960/960 strict-valid. This is structural enforcement, not learned protocol improvement. Unconstrained combined broadcast validity was 475/960.

Throughput fell from 455.196 to 198.765 completion tokens/s. Generated-minus-dropped effect was +0.975, 95% CI [-0.0125, 1.6125], p=0.25, versus +3.15 unconstrained; too small for a strategic claim. Both policies emitted nonempty broadcasts about 40 percent of the time and spent near the communication budget under constraints, so validity cannot be interpreted as learned messaging competence.
